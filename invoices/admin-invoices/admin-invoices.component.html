Help Center

Invoice Help

This page allows administrators to manage all invoices, track payment statuses, and generate new invoices.

Key Features

Invoice Tabs

  • Unpaid Invoices – View and manage outstanding payments
  • Paid Invoices – Review completed transactions

Search & Filter

Quickly find invoices by:

  • Invoice number
  • Job title
  • Customer name
  • Company name
  • Completion date

Management Tools

  • Create New Invoices – Generate invoices for completed jobs
  • Edit Invoice Details – Update amounts or re-upload documents
  • Update Payment Status – Mark invoices as paid
  • View Invoice PDFs – Access and download invoice documents

How to Use

Creating a New Invoice

  1. Click the button
  2. Select the completed job from the list
  3. Enter invoice details (number, amount, etc.)
  4. Upload the invoice PDF document
  5. Click Submit to generate the invoice

Editing an Invoice

  1. Click the button on the invoice row
  2. Update the invoice details as needed
  3. Replace the PDF document if required
  4. Click Update Invoice to save changes

Marking as Paid

  1. Click the button
  2. Confirm the payment status change
  3. The invoice will automatically move to the "Paid" tab

Troubleshooting

"PDF link not available" – Try re-uploading the invoice document

Upload errors – Ensure files are PDF format and under 10MB

Missing invoices – Verify the job has been marked as completed
