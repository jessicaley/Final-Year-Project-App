Payment Help

How to Pay Your Invoice

Online Payment (Recommended)

  1. Click "Pay Online" next to your invoice
  2. You'll be redirected to our secure payment portal
  3. Enter your credit/debit card details
  4. Complete the payment - it's instant and secure
  5. Your invoice will be marked as paid automatically

Bank Transfer (EFT)

  1. Click "View Invoice" to see our banking details
  2. Make a transfer using your online banking
  3. Click "Upload EFT Proof" to submit your proof of payment
  4. Select your bank statement screenshot or PDF
  5. We'll verify your payment within 24-48 hours

Need Help?

If you encounter any issues with payment:

  • Ensure you have sufficient funds
  • Check that your card supports online payments
  • Contact your bank if payments are being declined

Please upload proof of your bank transfer (EFT). Accepted formats:

  • Screenshot of online banking confirmation
  • PDF bank statement
  • Photo of deposit slip
