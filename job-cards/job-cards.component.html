 Help Center

Job Cards Help

This page allows you to track and manage all job cards throughout their lifecycle - from pending to completion.

Key Features

Status Tracking

  • Pending - Jobs awaiting processing
  • In Progress - Active jobs being worked on
  • Completed - Successfully finished jobs
  • Cancelled - Jobs that were cancelled

Search & Filter

Quickly find job cards by:

  • Job card ID
  • Customer name
  • Company name
  • Phone number

Job Management

  • Create New Job Cards - Convert quotations to work orders
  • View Full Details - See all job information and documents
  • Update Status - Track job progress through workflow
  • View Designs - Access design files and images

How to Use

Creating a Job Card

  1. Click in top right
  2. Select the quotation to convert to a job card
  3. Assign patterns and other required details
  4. Set the initial status (typically "Pending")
  5. Click Submit to create the job card

Viewing Job Details

  1. Click the action button
  2. The full job card view will open with all details
  3. Access related documents (quotations, designs, etc.)
  4. Click Back to Jobs to return to the list

Updating Job Status

  1. Click the action button
  2. Select the new status from the dropdown
  3. Add any relevant notes about the status change
  4. Click Update to save changes

Viewing Design Files

  1. Click the button
  2. A gallery will open showing all design images
  3. Browse through the images using the navigation controls
  4. Close the gallery when finished

Troubleshooting

Missing designs? Ensure the job request has design files uploaded

Can't create job card? Verify the quotation exists and is approved

Status not updating? Check you have proper permissions

For further assistance, contact your system administrator.
