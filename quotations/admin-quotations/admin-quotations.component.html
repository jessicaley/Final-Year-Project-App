How to manage quotations

This page allows you to view and manage all quotations in the system.

  • Pending: Quotations that have been created but not yet accepted or rejected by the customer
  • Accepted: Quotations that have been accepted by the customer
  • Rejected: Quotations that have been rejected by the customer

You can use the search box to filter quotations by any field including quotation number, job title, or customer name.

For accepted quotations, you can create an invoice unless one has already been created (indicated by the blue tag).
